# 4 Ristoranti — Show Format Reference

*Alessandro Borghese — 4 Ristoranti* (Sky Uno / TV8, 2015–present)  
Adapted from the German format *Mein Lokal, Dein Lokal*  
11+ seasons as of 2025–2026

---

## The premise

Four restaurant owners sharing a common trait — cuisine type, geographic area, or concept — compete to be named the best of the four. They do this by visiting and secretly scoring each other, under the supervision of celebrity chef Alessandro Borghese.

No external judges. No studio audience. The rivals are the jury.

---

## Episode structure

Each episode covers all four restaurant visits in sequence.

```
1. ARRIVAL        — Borghese drives to the area in his iconic black van
2. INTRODUCTION   — The four owners introduce their restaurants and make their case
3. VISIT × 4      — Each restaurant is visited in turn (see below)
4. IL CONFRONTO   — All scores revealed simultaneously; Borghese's vote added; winner declared
5. DEPARTURE      — Borghese leaves in the van
```

---

## Each restaurant visit

```
a. Kitchen inspection   — Borghese enters the kitchen alone, checks standards before sitting
b. The meal             — All four owners + Borghese eat together as guests
c. Secret scoring       — Each owner privately scores the host restaurant (not revealed yet)
```

The host owner cannot score their own restaurant. Borghese scores independently and his vote is kept sealed until *il confronto*.

---

## Scoring system

### The five categories

| # | Category | Italian | What is being judged |
|---|----------|---------|----------------------|
| 1 | Location | Location | Space, setting, ambience, exterior |
| 2 | Menu | Menu | Food quality, originality, execution |
| 3 | Service | Servizio | Staff, welcome, pace, professionalism |
| 4 | Bill | Conto | Value for money — price relative to experience |
| 5 | Special | Special | A local ingredient or typical preparation — changes every episode |

### Scale

- **0 to 10** per category (seasons 2 onwards; season 1 used 0–5)
- The Special category was introduced from season 6, episode 11, replacing earlier bonus points

### Borghese's role

- Borghese scores independently, on the same five categories
- His vote is **not revealed** during the score comparison — it's added last
- It can **confirm or overturn** the standing produced by the owner votes
- This exists explicitly to counter strategic voting (owners tend to underrate their rivals)

---

## The strategic voting problem

Because owners score competitors, there is a strong incentive to give low scores tactically — suppressing rivals rather than rating honestly. This is a known feature of the format, not a bug. The audience watches for it. Borghese's overturning power is the structural corrective.

---

## The prize

The episode winner takes home **€5,000** to invest in their own restaurant. There is no cumulative ranking across episodes — each episode is self-contained.

## Iconic elements

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| The black van | Borghese's vehicle between locations — a visual signature of the show |
| *Il confronto* | The reveal scene: all scorecards shown side by side, tension built before Borghese's vote |
| Kitchen inspection | Borghese alone in the rival's kitchen — the moment of professional judgment |
| *"Posso ribaltare tutto"* | Borghese's line: *"I can overturn everything"* — the reminder of his authority |

## How the talk adapts this format

| Show element | Talk adaptation |
|-------------|----------------|
| 4 competing restaurants | 4 AI coding methods (Autocomplete+, Vibe, Agent as junior dev, Spec-driven) |
| Each owner hosts rivals | Each paradigm gets its own section where it makes its best case |
| Kitchen inspection | The technical substance of each coding method |
| Competitor reviews | Fake reviews from developer personas (honest, not strategic) |
| La Pagella / *il confronto* | KPI scorecard at the end of each section |
| Borghese's overturning vote | The KPI matrix replaces a single expert verdict |
| No cumulative winner | No method wins — audience picks based on their context |
| The black van | Slide transitions between restaurant sections |

### Key divergence from the real format

In the show, rivals score each other — creating bias. In the talk, the reviews are from neutral developer personas. The strategic-voting tension is removed because the goal is clarity, not drama.

The real Borghese can overturn the jury. In the talk, no one overturns anything — the matrix is the final word.
